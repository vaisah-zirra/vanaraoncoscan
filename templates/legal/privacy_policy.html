This policy explains how Vanara Multi-Ventures (“Vanara”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses personal data when you visit our website, request a demonstration, join the pilot or waitlist, or use the VanaraOncoScan workspace. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (“NDPA”).

2. What we collect

  • Account and contact details: your name, email address, organisation and professional role. Your role is self-reported and is not independently verified.
  • Interest and contact forms: the details and message you send us. WhatsApp contact is optional and only used with your permission.
  • Workspace activity: when you start a case, attach an image, run a quality check, open an assessment, record a care decision or request a PDF report.
  • Content you add: test case records, ultrasound images and notes you enter in the workspace, and the reports generated from them.
  • Feedback: your ratings, adoption concerns, suggestions and chosen next step.
  • Visit measurement: first-party session records showing visits and campaign sources (for example Instagram or WhatsApp). A new visit begins after 30 minutes of inactivity. These records do not include IP addresses, browser fingerprints or patient content.
  • Guest-link access: if you enter through a review link, we record the organisation or group the link was issued to.

3. Patient and health information

Health information is sensitive personal data under the NDPA and receives a higher level of protection. The pilot is designed to run on supplied test records only. Please do not upload or type real patient names, identifiers, scans or clinical details. If real patient information is entered by mistake, tell us straight away at info@vanaraoncoscan.cyou so we can remove it.

4. Why we use your data and our legal basis

  • To create and run your account, and keep you signed in. Basis: performing the service you asked for.
  • To evaluate the pilot, improve the workflow and understand how the platform is used. Basis: our legitimate interests, and your consent where you give it.
  • To respond to demo, pricing, partnership and support requests. Basis: your request and our legitimate interests.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep the platform secure and meet legal obligations. Basis: legal obligation and legitimate interests.

Submitting an interest or waitlist request does not enrol you in a paid subscription. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Cookies and visit measurement

We use essential session cookies that keep you signed in and protect forms from misuse. These are necessary for the site to work. We also use first-party session records to count visits and walkthrough completion. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. If we add non-essential cookies in future, we will ask for your consent first.

6. Who we share data with

Registered activity and feedback are linked to your account and are visible to authorised Vanara team members in our private validation dashboard. We may share data with service providers that host or support the platform, who may only use it on our instructions and under confidentiality duties. We may also disclose data where the law requires it or to protect rights and safety.

7. Transfers outside Nigeria

Our hosting or service providers may store or process data outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we take steps to make sure your data stays protected in line with the NDPA.

8. How long we keep data

We keep account and pilot records while your account is active and for up to [24 months] after the pilot ends or your last activity, unless the law requires longer. Visit measurement records are kept in aggregated form for up to [24 months]. Contact and waitlist requests are kept for up to [24 months] unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

9. Security

We use access controls, authenticated sessions and restricted internal access to protect your data. No online service is completely secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and affected people as the NDPA requires.

10. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold your data and give you a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • delete your data, or restrict how we use it;
  • stop processing based on our legitimate interests, or withdraw consent you gave earlier;
  • provide your data in a portable format where applicable.

To make a request, email info@vanaraoncoscan.cyou from your registered address or use the contact form. Please do not include patient information. If you are unhappy with our response, you have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

11. Children

VanaraOncoScan is for healthcare professionals and facility teams. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the pilot develops. The effective date above shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we will tell registered users.

13. Contact
