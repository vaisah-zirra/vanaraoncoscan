THE PLATFORM

More than a scan.
A complete case.

Most tools stop at the image. VanaraOncoScan follows the whole case: the reason for the visit, the scan, the AI findings, a specialist’s opinion and the care plan, so nothing gets lost between steps.

AI reads the scan.

Upload a breast ultrasound. VanaraOncoScan first checks that the image is clear enough to read, then marks any areas of concern and drafts a structured report. Each finding is given a BI-RADS category, the standard scale radiologists use, together with a confidence score.

02 / SECOND OPINION
◎

A specialist when it matters.

When the AI is less certain, the case is sent to a radiologist working remotely (teleradiology), who signs a second opinion. Clinicians can request one for any case.

Follow the workflow ↗
03 / ACT
↗

Agree the next step.

The clinician confirms the care plan: routine care, follow-up or referral. Referrals and follow-ups stay tracked, and the whole case is saved as one PDF report.

Ask us something else →
What is VanaraOncoScan?+

A web platform for breast ultrasound. It helps clinics review scans with AI support, get a specialist’s second opinion when needed, and record the right next step for each patient, with one clear case report at the end.

What is BI-RADS?+

BI-RADS is the standard scale radiologists use to describe breast imaging results. It runs from 0 (more imaging needed) to 6 (cancer already confirmed by biopsy). Higher numbers mean more concern, and each category comes with a recommended next step.

Who gives the second opinion?+

A radiologist working remotely reviews the scan and the AI findings, then signs a report with their own opinion and recommendation. Cases where the AI is less certain are sent automatically, and clinicians can request a second opinion for any case.

Can I try it?+

Yes. Create a free account to get your own workspace with sample patients and scans. You can log in again any time and carry on where you left off.

Does it diagnose breast cancer?+

No. The AI highlights findings and suggests a BI-RADS category, but a clinician always makes the decision, with a specialist’s input when needed. Only a biopsy can confirm cancer. VanaraOncoScan is investigational software, and the preview must not be used to diagnose or manage real patients.

How do subscriptions work?+

Choose Practice, Clinic or Network and tell us about your needs. We will agree access and pricing with you directly. There is no online checkout yet.

Can we discuss a partnership?+

Yes. We welcome clinical teams, imaging centres, healthcare organisations and research partners. Choose “Partnership or research” in the form below.
