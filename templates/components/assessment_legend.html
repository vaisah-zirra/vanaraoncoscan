Ring fill shows the displayed classification confidence; the gap is the remainder to 100%. Confidence expresses the model’s certainty in the displayed classification; it is not the probability of cancer. Colours describe the impression, not confidence thresholds. Green outlines on ultrasound images mark regions of interest and do not indicate a benign result.
