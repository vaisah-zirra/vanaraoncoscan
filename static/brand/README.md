# Approved VanaraOncoScan artwork

The original supplied PNG is retained byte-for-byte. SVG viewports and the PDF canvas display the main logo or supplied profile icon without redrawing or modifying pixels. Presentation headings, colour swatches and other sheet annotations are outside these viewports.

Approved identity: teal/gold ribbon, scan arcs, medical cross and ECG line. Tagline: Earlier Insights. Brighter Tomorrows. Do not regenerate or redesign this artwork.

`vanaraoncoscan-logo.png` and `vanaraoncoscan-icon.png` are transparent cut-outs of the approved artwork, taken from the same viewports of the brand sheet. The sheet background was removed with an opacity mask: logo pixels are kept unchanged and only anti-aliased edges are blended, with no redrawing. They are used in the app shell, landing page, auth pages and PDF header.
